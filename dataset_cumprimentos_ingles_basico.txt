# MINI DATASET - CUMPRIMENTOS E INGLÊS BÁSICO
# Formato: pergunta/frase -> resposta esperada
# Objetivo: treino inicial de um modelo de linguagem "recém-nascido"

## SAUDAÇÕES (GREETINGS)
Hello! -> Hi! How are you?
Hi! -> Hello! Nice to meet you.
Good morning! -> Good morning! Did you sleep well?
Good afternoon! -> Good afternoon! How is your day?
Good evening! -> Good evening! Are you having dinner?
Good night! -> Good night! Sleep well.
Hey! -> Hey! What's up?
Howdy! -> Howdy! Good to see you.

## APRESENTAÇÕES (INTRODUCTIONS)
What is your name? -> My name is Anna.
Nice to meet you. -> Nice to meet you too.
How old are you? -> I am ten years old.
Where are you from? -> I am from Brazil.
What do you do? -> I am a student.

## COMO ESTÁ (HOW ARE YOU)
How are you? -> I am fine, thank you.
How are you doing? -> I am doing great!
Are you okay? -> Yes, I am okay.
How is everything? -> Everything is good.

## CORTESIA (POLITENESS)
Please. -> Of course.
Thank you. -> You're welcome.
Thanks a lot. -> No problem.
Excuse me. -> Yes, how can I help?
I'm sorry. -> That's okay.
Can you help me? -> Sure, I can help you.

## DESPEDIDAS (FAREWELLS)
Goodbye! -> Goodbye! Take care.
Bye! -> Bye! See you soon.
See you later! -> See you later!
See you tomorrow! -> See you tomorrow!
Take care! -> You too, take care!
Have a nice day! -> Thank you, you too!

## RESPOSTAS BÁSICAS (BASIC ANSWERS)
Yes. -> Yes, that's right.
No. -> No, that's not right.
Maybe. -> Maybe, I'm not sure.
I don't know. -> That's okay, let's find out.

## NÚMEROS (NUMBERS)
one -> 1
two -> 2
three -> 3
four -> 4
five -> 5
six -> 6
seven -> 7
eight -> 8
nine -> 9
ten -> 10

## DIAS DA SEMANA (DAYS OF THE WEEK)
Monday -> segunda-feira
Tuesday -> terça-feira
Wednesday -> quarta-feira
Thursday -> quinta-feira
Friday -> sexta-feira
Saturday -> sábado
Sunday -> domingo

## CORES (COLORS)
red -> vermelho
blue -> azul
green -> verde
yellow -> amarelo
black -> preto
white -> branco

## FRASES CURTAS DE REFORÇO (SHORT REINFORCEMENT SENTENCES)
Hello, my name is Tom.
Hi, how are you today?
Good morning, everyone!
Thank you very much.
See you tomorrow, bye!
Nice to meet you, goodbye.
